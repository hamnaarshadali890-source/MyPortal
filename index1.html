Instant AI First Aid Guidance

Access instant step-by-step emergency first-aid protocols driven by AI voice and text search. Clear guidance when every second counts.

Critical

Adult CPR Protocol

For unresponsive victims who are not breathing normally.

  1. Check Scene Safety: Ensure environment is safe for responder.
  2. Call 1122: Alert emergency services immediately.
  3. Chest Compressions: Push hard and fast in center of chest (100–120 bpm).
Critical

Choking Response

Action plan when airway is completely blocked.

  1. 5 Back Blows: Deliver firm blows between shoulder blades.
  2. 5 Abdominal Thrusts: Inward/upward thrusts above navel.
  3. Repeat: Alternate until obstruction clears or victim loses consciousness.
Urgent

Severe Bleeding

How to control major arterial or venous hemorrhages.

  1. Direct Pressure: Apply firm, constant pressure with sterile cloth.
  2. Elevate: Keep wounded limb above heart level if no fracture.
  3. Tourniquet: Apply 2 inches above wound if severe bleeding continues.
Urgent

Burns Treatment

First-response care for heat, chemical, or electrical burns.

  1. Cool Burn: Run cool water for 10–20 mins. Never use ice or butter.
  2. Cover Cleanly: Wrap loosely with sterile cling film or non-stick gauze.
  3. Seek Care: Get immediate help for facial or deep 2nd/3rd degree burns.
